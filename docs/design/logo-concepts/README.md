# Wasel A.I. logo concepts

Open `index.html` in a browser to view the responsive concept sheet, or open `logo-concepts.png` for the static image.

## Directions

| # | Name | Idea |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | **Route W** | A route line becomes a W, with an origin dot and a destination ring. Reads as a map, an initial, and a stop. |
| 02 | **Wa** | The Arabic letter leads. A destination dot turns it into a stop on the network. |
| 03 | **Arrival** | A route rises toward a coral arrow: the moment the app answers. |
| 04 | **Path & stops** | A softer route with start, transfer, and destination nodes. |
| 05 | **Voice loop** | A speech bubble with a waveform. Puts the voice-first interaction at the center. |
| 06 | **Bilingual wordmark** | A type-led lockup with Arabic leading and English supporting. |

## Recommendation

Use **01 Route W** as the primary mark, paired with the bilingual wordmark. It is the most flexible direction:

- Reads as a route, a W, and a bus stop.
- Stays legible at 24–32 px.
- Works in one color, on light and dark surfaces.
- Extends naturally to the app icon, favicon, and loading state.

Use **05 Voice loop** as a secondary illustration for voice-help moments, not as the primary brand mark.

The recommended light and dark lockups are shown at the bottom of the concept sheet.

## Selected

**04 Path & stops** is the chosen mark. It is now used in the site header, footer, welcome screen, favicon, app icons, and loading splash. The three nodes read as origin, transfer, and destination.
